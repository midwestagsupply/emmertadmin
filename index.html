Build notes, for our meeting only

  • This is a mockup of the screen, not a working backend yet. The point is to agree what staff can change before anything gets built. Right now that is four things: today’s hours, the notice banner, the regular weekly hours and the small print. Nothing else on the site is editable, on purpose, because every extra field is another thing that can go wrong at 6am in October.
  • How it would work. A plain HTML form posting to a small server, which writes the page. No dashboard software to learn, no page builder, nothing to break the layout. It works on the office computer and on a phone from the scale house.
  • Prices are deliberately not on this screen. Those come straight from the Barchart feed. If staff had to key prices in by hand every morning they would go stale, and a wrong price on a website is worse than no price.
  • On a scrolling banner. The yellow bar is static on purpose. Moving text is genuinely hard to read, it cannot be paused, and it is the first thing people complain about. If they want it to scroll after seeing it, that is easy to add, but I would push back once.
  • Still to decide: who gets a login and how many, whether changes need a second person to approve, and whether we keep a simple history of who changed what and when. I would keep the history, it costs almost nothing and settles arguments.
